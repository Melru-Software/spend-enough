How it works

Your numbers, run through every market since 1926.

You enter what you have, what comes in, what goes out, and when you'd like to stop working, then pick a decision to test. Every assumption behind the result is on the screen and yours to change: the stock/bond mix, fees, tax rates, spending guardrails. The tool runs your numbers two ways. Historical mode replays your plan through every stretch of real US market returns since 1926, so you can see how it would have gone if you'd started in 1929, or 1966, or 2000. Simulated mode runs 500 randomized futures with the average return and volatility of your stock/bond mix, both of which you can change. Both give you a range instead of a single line. A plan that comes through 66 of 71 market histories is a different thing from one that squeaks through 40, and you can see which one you're looking at.

What it isn't

Not advice. Not a planner.

It's not advice and it's not a planner. It doesn't know your taxes beyond a rough estimate, and it doesn't know your life. It's a way to see the shape of the outcomes so you can have a better conversation, with yourself or with a professional.

It doesn't model healthcare costs before Medicare, required minimum distributions, or Roth conversions. If you're optimizing those, you want a subscription planner. If you're deciding whether to take the year off, or whether you can spend a little more now, you want this.
