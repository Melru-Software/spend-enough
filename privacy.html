Privacy policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Who we are

Spend Enough is made by Melru Software LLC. You can reach us at support@spendenough.com.

The short version

The app runs in your browser. The numbers you enter never leave your device. We do not have accounts, so we do not have a database of users. If you buy the paid version, our payment processor collects your email and payment details, and we receive your email address and a license key. We use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to count page views and a handful of events so we can tell whether the app is working for people. That is the whole list.

What we collect, and why

Your financial inputs. Savings, income, spending, ages, and everything else you type into the app are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device so they are still there when you come back. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them. If you save a scenario, it stays in your browser unless you choose to share it.

Your license key. When you unlock the paid version, the key is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. When you enter a key, and about once a day afterwards, the app sends the key (and only the key) to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it is still valid. Nothing you type into the planner is included.

Your email address, at purchase only. If you buy the paid version, Lemon Squeezy collects your email address, payment method, and billing details. We receive your email address, the product you bought, and your license key. We use your email to deliver the license key, send receipts, and handle refunds and support. We do not add you to a marketing list unless you separately ask to be on one.

Analytics. We use [ANALYTICS PROVIDER] to count page views and a small number of events (for example, that a scenario was run, that the unlock screen was shown, or that a purchase completed). The intent is that this data cannot identify you and does not include any of the numbers you enter.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not have your financial inputs, so we cannot see, share, or lose them.
  • We do not run advertising or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not build a profile of you.

Third parties

We rely on a few services to run the product. Each has its own privacy policy that governs the data it handles directly.

  • Lemon Squeezy (planned: Lemon Squeezy), which processes payments, collects sales tax as the merchant of record, and delivers license keys.
  • [ANALYTICS PROVIDER], for anonymous usage counts.
  • Netlify, which serves the site.

Where data is kept and for how long

Your inputs stay in your browser until you clear them. Purchase records (email, product, license key) are kept for as long as we need them to support your license, comply with tax and accounting law, and handle refunds and disputes. Analytics data is kept according to [ANALYTICS PROVIDER]'s retention policy.
